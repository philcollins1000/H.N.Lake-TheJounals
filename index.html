    The Naval Journal
    of H.N. Lake

    20th February 1914 – 29th April 1915

    A first-hand account of the Grand Fleet at the outbreak of the First World War — written aboard HMS Antrim by a young naval officer on the eve of his 21st birthday.

    Twelve volumes spanning peacetime exercises, the declaration of war, a collision at sea, the sinking of three British cruisers, a torpedo attack, the Scarborough Raid, and the Battle of Dogger Bank.

    Transcribed June 2026 for the Lake family
